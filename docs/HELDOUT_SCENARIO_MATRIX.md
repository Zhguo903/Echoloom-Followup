Every generated item remains `draft` and `needs_human_review` until two human review decisions are recorded outside the coding-agent workflow.

## Drafting constraints

Each of the 48 items contains six synthetic candidate cards: one useful/current card, one topically similar low-value card, one stale/conflicting card, one wrong-branch cross-domain card, one permissioned sensitive card, and one clearly irrelevant card. At least three should remain superficially plausible to a relevance-only baseline.

The four profiles mean only that the authoring hypothesis is centered on `SCOPED_IMPLICIT`, `IGNORE`, `SCOPED_EXPLICIT`, or `ASK_FIRST`. They are not human labels. Candidate order is deterministic and recorded in the manifest.

Cross-cutting tags are assigned so the automated report can check the minimum targets in `runbook_phase2.md`. Automated coverage is a structural check; it cannot certify naturalness, neutrality, ambiguity, or research validity.
