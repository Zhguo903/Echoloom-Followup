# Claim Boundaries

This prototype operationalizes design requirements; it does not establish universal human preferences.

- Do not claim that people naturally use a fixed four-factor model.
- Six 2026 interviews cannot estimate prevalence or market percentages.
- Keep 2026 interviews separate from 2023 Replika material.
- `IGNORE`, `SCOPED_IMPLICIT`, `SCOPED_EXPLICIT`, and `ASK_FIRST` are actions to test.
- The four requirements are a synthesis from formative evidence and prior work.
- Do not publish interview quotations until consent and ethics routes are confirmed.
- Customer-discovery surveys are not academic participant data.
- Reconsider-Lite neither simulates human cognition nor guarantees safety.
- Null, mixed, and heterogeneous outcomes are valid and must remain reportable.
