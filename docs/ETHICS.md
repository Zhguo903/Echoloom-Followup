# Ethics and Privacy

## Existing-data fork

The consent and institutional status of the six 2026 interviews and separate 2023 material must be confirmed before academic secondary analysis. If permitted, excerpts require anonymization, coding documentation, negative-case review, and formative—not generalizable—reporting. If not permitted, they remain internal design input; publish no quotations or participant-data claims. Customer-discovery surveys are not silently repurposed.

## Repository and demo policy

Only mild, synthetic, non-clinical scenarios ship. There is no private-chat upload, hidden remote logging, diagnosis, therapy, guilt for absence, consciousness claim, dependency framing, or pressure to disclose. Local SQLite and minimal metadata are defaults. Raw prompts/provider payloads are not logged by default.

## Later response study

Study mode stays off until an appropriate ethics/consent pathway is confirmed. The proposed study is adult-only, blinded, minimal-data, and supports skip, withdrawal, debriefing, and deletion. It collects generated participant codes rather than names/emails, and never requests private chats, clinical status, or sensitive disclosures.

## Deployment restrictions

Use server-side keys, localhost CORS by default, redacted logs, CSP at the deployment proxy/app boundary, input limits, same-site cookies if introduced, and reverse-proxy rate limits. Do not expose admin endpoints publicly without production authentication. A successful mock test is not a safety validation.
